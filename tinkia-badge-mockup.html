The red is a NEW colour and deliberately not --sold. --sold is a muted maroon #8A3E4E reserved for gone; this is a vivid #D22B2B. They must not be confusable — one says this is over, the other says make me an offer. Different hue, much higher saturation, and they never appear together, since a hidden item has no card.

It is a separate axis, not a fourth segment. An item can be for sale AND open to haggling, or in negotiation and still open to it. So the seller row keeps the three-state control and gains one independent toggle below it — pressed state in the same red, so the control and the badge are visibly the same fact.

Tilted ~9° with a drop shadow so it reads as stuck on rather than drawn in. That is the sticker idiom doing the work; upright and flat it would look like a system label.

Prices now show on the seller page, at the user's request — reversing my earlier "no prices". They help the seller identify the right item at a glance, which matters more than the tidiness of leaving them out.
