Two oscillators, 512 waves, one panel.

The mk3 is the version people were waiting for: full 16-bit output, both oscillators genuinely independent, and the wavetables laid out on faders rather than buried in a menu. Move a slider and the timbre moves with it.

Feed the audio input and it stops being an oscillator. The selected wavetable becomes a waveshaper with voltage-controlled drive — same panel, second instrument.
