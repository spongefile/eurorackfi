What the swap buys

The band ignores the filters. Filtering to Sampo changes the count line to “20 / 86” and leaves Toiveet byte-identical — still Kalle, Sampo and spongefile. So it currently sits inside the filtered region while being immune to it. Filter to one person and the other two people’s wishes stay sitting directly under the bar you just used.

It separates the filter bar from its own results. The count line is the filter bar’s own output and is pushed 224px below it by an unrelated section.

The sticky bar eats it. Toiveet sits immediately under a sticky element, so it is the first thing to slide underneath and disappear as you scroll — the worst spot on the page for something meant to be noticed.

What it costs

The filter bar drops from 485px to 709px, so on a laptop it lands at the fold rather than comfortably above it. The bar is sticky, so one scroll pins it — and with the new desktop behaviour it then stays open. But the first thing a visitor meets becomes the rack and the wishlists rather than the controls.

Which may be the better introduction: here is who we are and what we are after, then the tools.
