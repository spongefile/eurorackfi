One choice, not two toggles. Three states that cannot overlap — for sale, someone is talking, gone — so it is one decision rather than two that interact. Every target is a full-width third of the row at 44px minimum, reachable one-handed.

The gloss sits once at the top, not on every row. "Hide" is the user's word and it is an action where the other two are states, so the sentence explaining that hiding is how you mark something sold does the work the label cannot — but twenty copies of it would be noise.

A sold row recedes but never moves. No reordering, no vanishing: the undo has to be exactly where the seller last saw the item, and something jumping away under the thumb is alarming when you have just tapped it by mistake.

No prices, no editing, no adding. The state is the whole job.
