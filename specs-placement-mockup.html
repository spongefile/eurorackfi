Specs sit with the photo, which is where they belong logically — both describe the object rather than the transaction. But it flips the imbalance rather than removing it: left becomes ≈700px, right ≈490px, so ~210px of gap moves to the right instead. That gap is under the CTA, which reads as breathing room rather than a hole, so it is still a net improvement — just not a fix.

One consequence to weigh: .imgcol is position:sticky. Making it ~700px tall means on a viewport shorter than that it can no longer pin usefully, and the handoff specifically warns about this column's sticky behaviour.
