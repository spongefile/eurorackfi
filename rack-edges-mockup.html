Three things stack into that dark band and make the figure read as recessed: 10px of dark case body above the top rail and below the bottom one, 1px #5c6169 borders on every rail top and bottom, and the outer 1px wood border — plus the drop shadow under all of it. Same modules and blanks throughout; only the edges change.

Current — 10px padding, dark rail borders, wood border

Dark above the top rail, dark below the bottom one, dark line either side of every rail. The figure sits in a well.

A — 5px padding, rail borders lightened to #6f757d

Halves the dark band and softens the rail edges. Still clearly a case, much less of a well. Smallest change of the three.

B — A, but rails get a highlight instead of borders

Rail edges become a light top and a soft bottom shade rather than two dark lines — the rail reads as a raised metal strip catching light, which is what it actually is, instead of being outlined.

C — B, and the outer wood border removed

Lightest. The cheeks do all the framing and nothing outlines the figure against the page. Risk: without any outer edge the dark body can look like it bleeds into the drop shadow at the corners — worth checking against the real page background rather than this one.
